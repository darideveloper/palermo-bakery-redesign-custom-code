## Requirements
### Requirement: Focused Centered Auth Form Layout
The system SHALL enforce a focused and centered layout for all authentication-related forms (Login, Register, Lost Password) generated by the Theme My Login plugin.

#### Scenario: Desktop Form Constrainment
- **WHEN** a user visits the `/login` or `/register` page on a viewport wider than 600px
- **THEN** the main form container SHALL be restricted to a maximum width of 600px
- **THEN** the form container SHALL be horizontally centered within its parent element
- **THEN** the form container SHALL have 50px of vertical margin at the top and bottom

#### Scenario: Mobile Form Responsiveness
- **WHEN** a user visits the authentication pages on a viewport narrower than 600px
- **THEN** the form container SHALL occupy the maximum available width of the content area
- **THEN** the form container SHALL maintain its vertical 50px margin to ensure separation from site headers and footers
